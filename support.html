AnonChat Support

Account, safety, and privacy help

Report or block someone

Use the Report control on a Timeline post, Community post, temporary-room message, or another user’s profile. Choose the closest reason and submit it for administrator review. Use Block on another user’s profile to stop new interaction between your accounts. Reports are not emergency services; if someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Delete your account

Use the public Delete account page to permanently remove your Firebase login and the AnonChat information your account controls. You will need your account email and password.

Get help without sharing credentials

For a bug, policy question, or access problem, use the project’s public support issue tracker. Do not post your password, authentication code, push-subscription keys, private messages, or other sensitive information there. Include only the details needed to reproduce the problem.

Policies

Read the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy for the service rules and data practices.

Need to leave AnonChat? Delete your account.
