How AnonChat Uses Your Data

Plain-language summary · Updated September 3, 2026

This page summarizes our current practices. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for the complete rules.

Information used to run AnonChat

AnonChat processes account email addresses through its authentication provider and stores pseudonymous usernames, profile choices, public posts, comments, reactions, follows, uploaded images, reports, subscription status, notification settings, and basic activity information such as timestamps and page usage.

Public information

Your pseudonymous username, public profile, posts, comments, reactions, follower relationships, badges, and other activity you choose to publish may be visible to other users. Your account email and password are not displayed to other users.

Encrypted chats

New private messages, temporary-room messages, and invite-only-room messages use end-to-end encryption after participants establish encryption keys. AnonChat stores encrypted message content and the routing information needed to operate chats, including participant identifiers, room membership, sender identifiers, timestamps, expiry information, and notification records. AnonChat does not normally possess the keys needed to read that encrypted content. Legacy messages created before encryption may remain in their original form until migrated or deleted.

Why information is processed

We use information to provide accounts and app features, deliver notifications, process subscriptions, prevent abuse, review public content and reports, secure the service, provide support, measure basic service activity, and meet legal obligations.

Service providers

Information may be processed by providers that operate AnonChat’s authentication, database, hosting, notifications, payments, and app distribution, including Firebase, Google, Google Play, and the payment provider identified at checkout. Their systems may process technical identifiers and network information under their own terms.

Legal and safety requests

AnonChat may preserve or disclose information available to it when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect users and investigate serious abuse. AnonChat cannot ordinarily provide end-to-end encrypted message content in readable form when it does not possess the participant-held decryption keys. Encryption does not conceal account identifiers, participants, room membership, timestamps, reports, or information retained by service providers.

Sales and advertising

AnonChat does not currently sell users’ personal information. If AnonChat’s practices change, this page, the Privacy Policy, and applicable app-store disclosures must be updated before the change applies.

Your choices

You can manage profile and notification choices, report or block users, remove content using available controls, and request permanent account deletion through the public Delete account page. Limited records may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.
