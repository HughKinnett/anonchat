Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

What AnonChat collects

AnonChat uses Firebase Authentication to process the email address and password you provide for sign-in. We store your account ID, pseudonymous username, profile information you choose to add, and the public and private user-generated content you create: posts, comments, reactions, follows, Community posts, temporary-room activity, message requests, direct messages, controlled-reveal choices, and related timestamps. We process images you choose to upload and the Spotify track link you choose to show on your profile.

If you enable browser notifications, we store the browser push-subscription endpoint and keys needed to send notifications. We record account activity and aggregate page-view/activity information to operate the service and show community analytics. Spotify embeds load from Spotify when you choose to add or view a Spotify track; Spotify’s handling is governed by its own policy.

How information is visible and used

Your username, public profile, public posts, comments, reactions, follows, Community posts, and selected Spotify track can be visible to other users. Direct messages and private profile details are for the participants you choose, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorized administrators can review public community activity, reports, moderation cases, and account health to keep the service safe; they do not receive passwords or Firebase authentication credentials.

Reports include the reporter, target, reason, and a protected moderation snapshot. Blocks are stored so the service can prevent new interaction between the blocked accounts. We use this information to provide accounts, content, safety controls, notifications, moderation, analytics, support, and legal compliance.

Retention and choices

Temporary rooms and their messages expire after 24 hours and are scheduled for deletion, although a protected moderation snapshot of reported material can be retained for review while the account remains active. Account deletion removes block relationships in both directions, report intakes submitted by or about the account, the account’s reporter attribution inside moderation cases, and moderation cases about the account. For about two hours after completion, a barrier document keyed by the deleted Firebase account ID retains only completion and expiry timestamps so a cached sign-in cannot recreate the account; it then automatically expires. Other information is retained while needed to operate AnonChat, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements. You can manage privacy choices and notification permission in the app, report or block users through in-app controls, and permanently remove your account through Delete account.

Contact

For privacy or account help, use AnonChat Support. This policy applies to AnonChat’s public website and installed web app.
