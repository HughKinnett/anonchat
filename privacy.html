Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

What AnonChat collects

AnonChat’s authentication provider securely processes the email address and password you provide for sign-in. AnonChat stores your account ID, pseudonymous username, profile information you choose to add, public posts, comments, reactions, follows, Community posts, message requests, controlled-reveal choices, and related timestamps. For end-to-end encrypted private messages, temporary-room messages, and invite-only room messages, AnonChat stores encrypted message bodies and encrypted photos plus the routing metadata needed to operate the service, including participants or room membership, sender identifiers, timestamps, expiry information, and delivery or notification records. We also process images you publish and the Spotify link you choose to display.

If you enable browser notifications, we store the browser push-subscription endpoint and keys needed to send notifications. We record account activity and aggregate page-view/activity information to operate the service and show community analytics. Spotify embeds load from Spotify when you choose to add or view a Spotify track; Spotify’s handling is governed by its own policy.

How information is visible and used

Your username, public profile, public posts, comments, reactions, follows, Community posts, and selected Spotify link can be visible to other users. Private profile details are shown only after the applicable controlled reveal. End-to-end encrypted chat contents are decrypted only on participant devices; authorized administrators and service providers do not receive the participant-held content-decryption keys. Administrators can review public activity, reports, available metadata, moderation cases, and account health, but they cannot decrypt encrypted chat contents.

Reports include the reporter, target, reason, and available metadata or public-content evidence. Reporting an encrypted room or user does not automatically give AnonChat the encrypted conversation contents. A participant may separately choose to disclose content, and recipients can save or capture content outside AnonChat. Blocks are stored so the service can prevent new interaction between blocked accounts. We use information available to us to provide accounts, routing, safety controls, notifications, moderation, analytics, support, and legal compliance. If legally required, AnonChat may disclose ciphertext and metadata it possesses, but it cannot disclose plaintext that it cannot decrypt.

Encryption, retention, and choices

Your encrypted-chat private key is stored only in password-wrapped form. Your chat encryption password is processed on your device and is not sent to AnonChat. Losing that password can make encrypted history permanently unrecoverable. Temporary rooms and their encrypted messages expire after 24 hours and are scheduled for deletion. Legacy content created before the encryption rollout may remain unencrypted until migrated or deleted. Account deletion removes the account and associated service records through the deletion process described in the Terms, although information copied or captured by another participant remains outside AnonChat’s control. Other ciphertext and metadata are retained while needed to operate AnonChat, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements. You can manage privacy and notifications, report or block users, and permanently remove your account through Delete account.

Contact
