Child Safety Standards

Effective September 3, 2026

Adults-only service

AnonChat is an anonymous social and chat service intended only for people age 18 or older. It does not target children. Age restrictions do not reduce our obligation to combat child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Zero tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

AnonChat prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, trafficking, sexualization of minors, and attempts to obtain or distribute such content. This includes real, illustrated, computer-generated, altered, or linked material when prohibited by law or platform policy.

Reporting

Users should use the in-app Report control immediately. Do not copy, download, forward, or publicly post suspected CSAM. For urgent danger, contact local emergency services. Private support requests must not include passwords or encryption keys.

Our response

AnonChat may promptly restrict access, remove content, suspend accounts, preserve relevant available records as required by law, and report apparent CSAM or child exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children or other appropriate authorities. End-to-end encryption limits AnonChat’s ability to inspect message contents unless a participant reports and discloses them; routing metadata and public content may still be available.

Compliance and contact

AnonChat commits to complying with applicable child-safety laws and maintaining a designated child-safety contact in Google Play Console. Until a dedicated private safety inbox is published, use the in-app reporting system for platform reports and Support for policy questions. Do not place sensitive evidence in a public issue.
