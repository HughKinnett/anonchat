Premium Subscription Terms

Effective September 3, 2026

Price and renewal

AnonChat Premium is offered at US $4.99 per month unless a different price is displayed before purchase. It automatically renews each billing period until canceled. Taxes and localized pricing may apply. Premium is optional; core free features remain available without a subscription.

Benefits

Premium currently includes eligible profile customization, Premium avatars and covers, ghost-mode controls, a Spotify playlist display, longer posts, and invite-only Premium rooms. Features may evolve, but a subscription must continue to provide meaningful recurring value.

Billing on Android and the web

Purchases made in the Google Play version must use Google Play Billing unless an authorized alternative-billing program applies. Google Play purchases, cancellation, renewal, and applicable refunds are managed through the user’s Google Play account. Purchases made independently on the AnonChat website may be processed by the payment provider identified at checkout and managed through that provider.

Cancellation and access

Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. Cancellation stops future renewal; Premium ordinarily remains active through the paid billing period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Deleting an AnonChat account also may not cancel billing automatically, so cancel through the store or payment provider first.

Founders and Founding Members

Accounts expressly designated by AnonChat as having lifetime Premium access do not require payment while that designation remains valid. It is not transferable or redeemable for cash.
