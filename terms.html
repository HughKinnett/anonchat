Encrypted chats, temporary rooms, and pseudonyms

New private messages, temporary-room messages, and invite-only room messages use end-to-end encryption after participants establish encryption keys. Message contents are encrypted on a participant’s device and can be decrypted only with participant-held keys. AnonChat does not hold a universal content-decryption key and cannot recover a forgotten chat encryption password. Encryption does not conceal account identifiers, room membership, participants, sender identifiers, timestamps, delivery records, reports, network information handled by service providers, or content a participant saves, captures, or voluntarily discloses. Legacy messages created before encryption may remain in their original form until migrated or deleted. Temporary rooms and their messages are designed to expire after 24 hours and are removed by scheduled cleanup. A pseudonymous handle does not guarantee that activity cannot be linked to you.
