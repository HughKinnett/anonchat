Copyright and Takedown Policy

Effective September 3, 2026

Users may upload only material they own or are authorized to share. AnonChat may remove allegedly infringing material and act against repeat infringers.

Copyright notice

A complete notice should identify the copyrighted work, identify and locate the material on AnonChat, provide the complaining party’s name and reliable contact information, state a good-faith belief that use is unauthorized, state under penalty of perjury that the notice is accurate and the sender is authorized to act, and include a physical or electronic signature.

Counter-notice

A person whose material was removed may submit identification of the removed material and its prior location, a statement under penalty of perjury that removal resulted from mistake or misidentification, their name and contact information, consent to the jurisdiction required by applicable law, acceptance of service from the original claimant, and a signature.

How to submit

Use AnonChat Support for current contact instructions. Do not publish addresses, identity documents, signatures, or other sensitive notice information in a public issue. A private, monitored takedown address and designated agent information must be added before relying on this page as a formal DMCA intake channel.

Fraudulent notices may create legal liability. Consider obtaining legal advice.
