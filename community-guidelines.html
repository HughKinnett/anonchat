Community Guidelines

Effective September 3, 2026

AnonChat supports candid pseudonymous conversation, not harm. These rules apply to profiles, posts, comments, reactions, messages, rooms, images, usernames, links, and other activity.

Do not exploit or endanger children

Never create, request, possess, share, promote, threaten to share, or link to child sexual abuse material or any sexual exploitation, grooming, trafficking, or endangerment of a minor. Apparent violations may result in immediate removal, account termination, preservation of relevant non-encrypted records where legally required, and reports to the appropriate authorities or designated child-protection organizations.

Do not threaten, abuse, or exploit people

  • No credible threats, incitement, stalking, coercion, extortion, trafficking, or instructions that facilitate serious harm.
  • No targeted harassment, hateful conduct, non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual exploitation, or encouragement of self-harm.
  • No impersonation intended to deceive, exposure of private identifying information, scams, malware, spam, or illegal sales.

Sexual and graphic material

Do not upload nudity or sexual content that violates applicable law or Google Play policy. Never post sexual content involving minors or people who did not consent. Content may be restricted, placed under review, or removed.

Authenticity and intellectual property

Post only material you have the right to share. Do not infringe copyright or trademarks, manipulate engagement, evade enforcement, or misrepresent dangerous content.

Reports and enforcement

Use the in-app Report control on content, rooms, messages, or profiles and Block controls when available. AnonChat may remove content, limit features, suspend or ban accounts, and preserve necessary records. Encrypted content cannot ordinarily be reviewed unless a participant voluntarily submits a readable copy with a report. Appeals may be submitted through Support.

Emergencies

AnonChat is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services when someone is in immediate danger.
